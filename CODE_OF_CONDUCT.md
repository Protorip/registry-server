# Code of conduct

Treat others with respect when participating in Protorip Registry Server. This policy applies to everyone, including the project maintainer, and covers all repository contributions and interactions, including issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and comments. It also applies when you represent the project.

## Expected behavior

- Welcome participants of all backgrounds and experience levels.
- Give and receive feedback constructively. Address the work or idea and keep criticism focused on it.
- Respect personal boundaries and ask permission before sharing another person's private information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, and publication of someone else's private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Report a concern

Email [hello@protos.rip](mailto:hello@protos.rip) to report a concern privately. The project maintainer receives reports sent to this address. Include what happened and, if available, links or screenshots that help explain it.

The maintainer will limit sharing of the report to people who need the information to review or act on it. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith or participates in a review is unacceptable.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews reports and decides what response fits the conduct, its impact, and any prior incidents. Responses may include removing content, issuing a warning, or temporarily or permanently restricting participation in project spaces.
